Hyperkalaemia Timeline Viewer

Data: MIMIC-IV Clinical Database Demo (100 patients, Open Database License). For demonstration purposes only.

A · Treatment map

Each row is an admission whose potassium went above 5.0 mmol/L, sorted by highest potassium. Each cell counts the IV doses of calcium, insulin or dextrose 50%, furosemide or sodium bicarbonate given during the stay. Click a row to open its timeline.

B · Admission timeline

Potassium results during the stay. The green band is the normal range (3.5–5.0 mmol/L) and red points are above 5.0. Purple triangles show when each IV treatment was given. For admissions marked ★, the blue line is the hyperkalaemia onset and the shaded area is the 2-hour treatment window from the in-hospital hyperkalaemia phenotype. ▲ marks admissions that first potassium was already above 5.0, so they are excluded from the phenotype.
